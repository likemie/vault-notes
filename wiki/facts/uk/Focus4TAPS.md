> [!claim] 项目定位
> 小学科学教师评价支持项目（Focus for Teacher Assessment in Primary Science，Focus4TAPS）面向小学科学学科负责人和任课教师；评估版本面向五年级。英国[[Education Endowment Foundation|教育捐赠基金会]]（Education Endowment Foundation，EEF）将其列为有望项目。[项目页](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/promising-programmes/focus-for-teacher-assessment-in-primary-science-focus4taps)

> [!program-context] 项目背景
> - **立项年份** <span class="eef-pending">待核实</span>
> - **实施范围** 学校教育。

---

> [!policy-design] 干预方案
> - **学习活动** 教师在科学实践探究中选取评价重点，结合学生自评与同伴评价调整教学支持，并在学年内覆盖不同探究技能。

[方案与实施安排](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/promising-programmes/focus-for-teacher-assessment-in-primary-science-focus4taps#nav-how-is-it-implemented)
