> [!claim] 项目定位
> 数学推理项目（Mathematical Reasoning）面向小学二年级学生。英国[[Education Endowment Foundation|教育捐赠基金会]]（Education Endowment Foundation，EEF）将其列为有望项目。[项目页](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/promising-programmes/mathematical-reasoning)

> [!program-context] 项目背景
> - **立项年份** <span class="eef-pending">待核实</span>
> - **实施范围** 学校教育。

---

> [!policy-design] 干预方案
> - **学习活动** 围绕数量关系和算术关系开展活动，结合教师指导与数学电脑游戏，着重理解加法组成及加减法互逆关系。

[方案与实施安排](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/promising-programmes/mathematical-reasoning#nav-how-is-it-implemented)
